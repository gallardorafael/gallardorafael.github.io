# Rafael Gallardo

I'm an applied scientist. These days I work on AI agents at Oracle, mostly on the tools, guardrails and workflows that help other engineers build agents they can trust.

I started in research at the [Language and Knowledge Engineering Laboratory](http://www.lke.buap.mx/) of [BUAP](https://www.buap.mx/), where I also studied computer science. After that I spent a few years on real-time computer vision at [Algotive](https://www.algotive.ai/), before moving to language models and agents.

## What I work on

- **Agentic engineering.** How to build software together with AI agents and still understand what got built: written specs, a person who approves each step, and decisions kept as files you can read later.
- **Agent frameworks and guardrails.** Making agents something a team can describe in configuration and run safely, even when nobody on the team is an ML specialist.
- **Inference and infrastructure.** On the side, I build and run the infrastructure that serves open models. This taught me a lot about inference engineering, model optimization and quantization, and deploying and scaling models on GPUs, both in the cloud and on our own hardware.
- **Computer vision, earlier on.** Fast inference on GPUs, small models for cheap devices, and following objects across many cameras.

## What I'm curious about

- How people and agents should share work, and who stays accountable for the result
- Open models that run on hardware you own
- Small, efficient models for places with little compute
- Multimodal models that connect language and vision
- Brain-computer interfaces, which I still read about when I can
